## Hi, I'm Bruno 👋

PhD student in **Space Geophysics** at the **National Institute for Space Research (INPE)**, Brazil, with a sandwich period at **Queen Mary University of London (QMUL)**.

I work on **3D reconstruction of the solar corona**: magnetic field extrapolation with Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) combined with Differential Emission Measure (DEM) inversion to infer the coronal plasma density.

🌐 **Website / CV:** [brunofernandesgarcia.github.io](https://brunofernandesgarcia.github.io/)
🌐 **Curriculo Lattes (pt-br):** (https://lattes.cnpq.br/7725799765396213)

### 🔭 Research interests
- Coronal magnetic fields: NLFFF and magnetohydrostatic (MHS) extrapolation
- Coronal plasma diagnostics: DEM inversion, density and temperature
- Physics-Informed Neural Networks and machine learning for heliophysics
- Solar flares and space weather (e.g., the May 2024 "Gannon" storm)
- Python
- Teaching Physics
